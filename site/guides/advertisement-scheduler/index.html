How to schedule adverts and jingles

The advertisement scheduler plays your adverts, jingles and station IDs by rules: after every few songs, every so many minutes, or at set times of day. Each advert can have its own run dates, and every play is logged for proof-of-play reports.

Open the scheduler

Choose Tools → Advertisement scheduler. It has two tabs:

  • 📋 Rules: what plays, how often, and when. Rules are listed on the left, and the one you pick opens on the right.
  • Adverts list: your adverts, each with the dates and days it may run. Rules can play from this list.

Good to know: adverts and jingles never count as songs, so a rule for "every 4 songs" counts music only.

1. Add your adverts (optional)

If you sell airtime, put each advert on the Adverts list: press ➕ Add advert, pick its audio file, and set the dates it runs from and to and the days of the week it may play. Each advert shows whether it's ● running today and which rules play it. When a campaign ends, the advert simply stops being picked; there's nothing to remember to remove.

For jingles and station IDs you don't need the list: a rule can play straight from a folder.

2. Make a rule

On the Rules tab press ➕ Advert rule or ➕ Jingle rule. A rule is set up in three steps, top to bottom.

What plays

  • From the Adverts list (advert rules): each advert plays only within its own dates and days. Best for paid adverts.
  • From a folder or chosen files: choose a folder, or press ➕ Add files to hand-pick files (chosen files are used instead of the folder). Best for jingles, IDs and promos.

Then choose how many to play each time and how to pick them: random, taking turns (each one in order, carrying on where it left off) or all of them.

3. Choose how often

TriggerExampleHow it plays
After every N songsAfter every 4 songs, play 1 jingleAdded straight after the song that starts.
Every N minutes or hoursEvery 30 minutes, play 2 advertsQueued after the song on air when the time comes; the song is never cut.
At set timesAt 08:58, 12:58 and 17:58Queued after the song on air at each time. Press ➕ Add time for more.

For minutes and hours, on the clock means the times line up with the clock: every 30 minutes on the clock plays at :00 and :30. With it off, the next one is 30 minutes after the last time the rule played.

4. Set when it's in force

  • Dates: from and to, with a calendar. Leave them empty to run all the time.
  • Days: tick days, or use Every day, Weekdays or Weekends.
  • Hours: all day, or from one hour until another. Ranges over midnight work, so from 22 until 6 covers the overnight show.

Each rule in the list shows ● in force now or ○ waiting (outside its dates, days or hours), and the On / off switch turns a rule off without losing it.

Test it

  • ▶ Play it now plays the rule's picks straight away.
  • Queue them after the song on air adds them as the next rows, as if the rule had just gone off.
  • Duplicate copies a rule, which is the quickest way to make a weekend version of a weekday rule.

More ways to use rules

  • In the playlist generator: a rule can be a pattern step or a timed insert, so generated playlists carry your jingles and adverts already.
  • From the event scheduler: the action Play adverts / jingles from a rule fires a rule at a set time, for example a sponsor's message before the news.
  • As a command: RULE Morning sponsors in a playlist command row, a plugin or an event queues that rule's files after the song on air.

Proof of play

Every advert and jingle that plays is recorded. In Tools → Reports, choose adverts or jingles and a period (this week, last month…), click an item to see every time it played, and export a proof of play PDF for the advertiser with every air time, or a CSV that opens in Excel.
